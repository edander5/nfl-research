# Team Total

**Observed.** The layout shares the weekly matchup strip, team header, line stepper, side selector, book offers, next games, filters, historical bars, game log and matchup context with Game Total. The selected side of the matchup supplies the team's scoring history. Its baseline for HOU vs IND was 28.5, Over 40% (4–6), Avg Points 24.9. L5/L10/L20/H2H and two season windows appear. The chart displays team points with dates/opponents in ascending chronology. The newest-first log labels the scoring column POINTS and includes SCORE, HIST LINE and O/U. For the Jan 18 playoff entry the historical line and O/U are both dashes. No period selector appeared in the baseline.

**Derived.** HOU at CLE yielded 24 team points, while the same event on Game Total yielded 51 combined. Four of the ten team scores exceeded 28.5 and the scores sum to 249. The missing historical line on the Jan 18 row did not prevent the 16-point score from appearing in the research sample. H2H interaction and ranking visibility are described in the [ledger](evidence/README.md). [Default screenshot](screenshots/team-total-desktop.png).

**Implementation.** Reuse the Game Total history and threshold components. Supply selected team ID, team-oriented score projection, event/opponent/venue, window, side, threshold and optional team-specific market snapshots. Select the other team to change the points series; retain a canonical matchup identity. Keep comparison context offense vs opposing defense. Acceptance: event orientation is correct when team selection flips, row-level historical data stays independent of the hypothetical threshold, and nullable historical lines render as unavailable. On small screens prioritize team identity, threshold and chart before the scrollable log. First-half support, H2H eligibility, hover, pushes and exact opponent-filter behavior remain open.
