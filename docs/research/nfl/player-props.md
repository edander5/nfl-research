**Implementation.** Essential discovery inputs are player/event identity, proposition, line/side, offer snapshots, opposition rank with definition, eligible sample counts and rates. Use typed search/filter/sort state and bounded result pages. Preserve research selection on detail entry and original discovery state on Back. Detail takes a stat mapping, eligible logs, threshold, historical snapshots, projected availability and optional opportunity data with denominators. At narrow widths put primary proposition and odds first, with scrollable or expandable metrics. Acceptance: search, side and sorting visibly update the listing, absent rates stay absent, details receive the selected proposition, and chart/log counts agree. Column preferences and saved presets are prior shipped-code findings; reload persistence, breakpoint boundaries, filter reset scope and gated controls are open.
