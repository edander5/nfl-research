# Moneyline / Spread

**Observed.** Week matchup strip → selected team → headline moneyline offers, Moneyline/Spread selector, MOVE, Save/Track → spread stepper and slider with alternate-line books → upcoming opponents → Filters and gated Advanced Opp. Filters → performance summary and game history → opponent analysis panels. The default HOU profile showed -12.5 and a slider labeled -25.5, 0 and +25.5. Visible windows were L5, L10, H2H, 2025 and 2026. The log labels WK, DATE, OPPONENT, SCORE, ML, MARGIN, ATSh and ATSc (with the selected line). Matchup comparisons include offense vs defense direction, season controls, man/zone pass splits, rushing scheme, line of scrimmage, and team usage. These labels are visible in the tree; access varies by panel.

Clicking Spread from the default Moneyline tab kept the URL and selected team. Market selection details and capture in the [ledger](evidence/README.md). [Default screenshot](screenshots/moneyline-desktop.png).

**Derived.** For HOU at CLE the score 24–27 gives a margin of −3. A historical spread of −4.5 is shown separately from the selected −12.5. The labels are consistent with historical and custom against-the-spread outcomes; historical line provenance is open. Define selected-team spread orientation explicitly in your application.

**Implementation.** Essential components are matchup/team selection, market switch, score-margin chart, spread line control, per-window W/L/ties and ATS outcomes, historical log, and one offense-vs-defense comparison. Inputs are team-oriented scores and margin, ML odds, selected spread, nullable historical spreads, eligibility and rank definitions. Show win/loss for Moneyline and cover/push/fail for Spread. Define covering as margin + selected spread > 0 in the proposed app. Acceptance: switching market retains team/event, integer ties and pushes are distinct, and historical lines retain their own labels and provenance. At narrow widths preserve accessible stepper and slider controls and make the log scrollable. Window transitions, slider increments, chart hover/row clicking and tie handling merit follow-up.
