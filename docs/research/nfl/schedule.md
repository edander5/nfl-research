# Schedule

**Observed.** Shared search → NFL Schedule → Week 1–18 buttons → date-grouped matchup cards. Week 11 showed 13 matchups. Upcoming cards showed kickoff in ET, broadcast label, stadium, team records, conference/division, moneyline prices, spread and total. Team logo and spread links point to team Moneyline / Spread. An O/U link points to the home team's Game Total route, for example /nfl/game-total/HOU for IND at HOU.

From the collapsed default state, clicking the first Matchup Details button expanded content on the same URL. The expanded tree included offense/defense context, positional fantasy points allowed with regular-season sample sizes, and subscription prompts for advanced analysis and betting splits. See the [ledger](evidence/README.md) and [default screenshot](screenshots/schedule-desktop.png).

**Implementation.** Essential components are week navigation, date sections, event cards, an accessible details disclosure and research links carrying explicit event and team identity. Return stable event ID, season/week, timestamp/time zone, status, venue, broadcast, teams, records, score and optional market snapshots. Week change replaces the event list. Expansion should load or reveal event context and preserve the schedule URL. On narrow screens, wrap market data while keeping away/home mapping explicit. Acceptance: users can choose a week, identify kickoff or final score, expand a single matchup and reach the appropriate market. Season picker, live clock/box score and missing-data/error displays remain open where unsampled. Avoid labeling an event final based solely on a score.

Week 10 replaced the upcoming cards with earlier fixtures marked FINAL, keeping the schedule URL. Matchup Details also includes venue/weather, team L10 records and H2H switches, offense-vs-defense ranks and DVOA. The explicit Half-PPR label and regular-season game counts apply to the positional fantasy section.
