## Important independent-design choices

The supplied Game Total example at 44.0 shows 67% (6–3–1), consistent with 6/(6+3); its mean remains 49.6. The image also shows one 44-point game below the current 44.5 line but graded favorably against its own 40.5 historical reference. These are distinct comparison bases, not a reason to merge columns or infer closing prices. The proposal will return explicit counts and denominator identifiers; owner approval is required for any new displayed rate convention.

Do not copy mixed-season/postseason samples: initial new market research preserves one selected season and explicit REG/POST, as existing trends do. L20/H2H/season, cross-season and combined-stage behavior are separate later scopes. Moneyline raw score ties are not automatically sportsbook pushes. No odds-implied percentage becomes a prediction; empirical frequency remains a retrospective hypothetical statistic.

No Save/Track/My Picks, bet placement, subscriptions, gated panels or trade-dress replication are proposed. Advanced DVOA, fantasy ranks, man/zone/scheme, red-zone, usage, first-half and verified closing-line panels remain unavailable until independently licensed/validated data and definitions are approved.
